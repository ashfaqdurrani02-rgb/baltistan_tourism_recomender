The Region

Why Baltistan?

Home to four of the world's fourteen eight-thousanders, ancient Silk Road forts, emerald lakes, and a culture untouched by time.

🏔

World-Class Mountains

K2, Broad Peak, Gasherbrum — the Karakoram crown rises here. Concordia is called the throne room of the mountain gods.

💧

Alpine Lakes & Glaciers

Satpara, Sheosar, Kachura — turquoise mirrors set among wild highlands. The Baltoro is one of Earth's longest non-polar glaciers.

🏰

Living Heritage

Shigar Fort, Khaplu Palace, and Kharpocho — restored Silk Road forts that host guests today as they did sultans centuries ago.

🌸

Deosai Plateau

At 4,114 m, the world's second highest plateau blooms with wildflowers every summer and shelters the rare Himalayan brown bear.
